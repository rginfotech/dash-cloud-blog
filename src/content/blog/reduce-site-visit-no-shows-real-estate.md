An agent drives to a property, and nobody shows up. It costs time, fuel and momentum. Most no-shows are not rejections. People forget, plans change, or they are unsure about the location.

## Why viewings get missed

- The booking was made days ago and has been forgotten
- Location or parking is unclear
- Something came up, and cancelling feels awkward
- The buyer was never really committed

## A reminder flow that works

| When | Message | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Right after booking | Confirmation with date, time, address and a map link | Sets the plan |
| 1 day before | Reminder, with a one-tap confirm or reschedule | Surfaces changes early |
| 2 hours before | Short reminder with the agent's name and contact | Prevents forgetting |
| After a no-show | Friendly offer to rebook | Recovers the lead |

## Make rescheduling easy

If cancelling takes effort, people just do not show up. Let them reply "reschedule" and offer two new slots immediately. A rescheduled visit is much better than an empty one.

## Where AI helps

An AI agent sends the messages, understands replies like "can we make it an hour later?", updates the calendar, and tells your agent. It also answers common questions, such as parking or what to bring, so the buyer arrives prepared.

## Confirm the serious ones

When a buyer confirms, mark them as committed. When they do not reply to the 1-day reminder, a person can call to check. That puts human effort where it matters.

## Measure it

Track the share of booked visits that happen, the reschedule rate, and how many no-shows rebook. Compare before and after you add reminders.

Always use only the contact details the buyer has shared, and make opting out simple.
