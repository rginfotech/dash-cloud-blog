Most agents spend their energy on buyer leads, but listings are the foundation of the business. Without properties to sell, there is nothing to show. Sellers often contact several agents, and the first helpful reply tends to set the tone.

## What a seller wants to know

- What is my property worth, roughly?
- How long might it take to sell?
- What will it cost me?
- Why should I choose you?

## A fast, useful first response

An AI agent can reply immediately, thank the seller and collect the details needed for a proper valuation: location, property type, size, condition and the reason for selling. Then it can book a call or visit with an agent.

## Be careful with valuations

An automated reply should never state a final price. Say that a valuation needs an agent to review the property and local sales. Share an honest, general process instead of a guess.

## Follow up with value

If the seller is not ready, do not drop them. Send short, relevant updates such as local market notes, tips on preparing a home or a note after a few weeks. Many sellers make decisions months after their first enquiry.

## Show proof, not promises

Share how you market properties, what a listing includes and examples of your work. Use only facts you can support, and avoid guaranteeing results or sale prices.

## Track your listing pipeline

Count seller enquiries, valuations booked, listings won and the source of each. Over time you will see which channels and responses produce results.
