Both voice agents and chatbots can answer questions and qualify leads. They fit different situations, and starting with the wrong one wastes time.

## What each one is best at

| | Chatbot (chat or WhatsApp) | Voice agent (phone) |
|---|---|---|
| Speed to launch | Faster | Needs more testing |
| Customer effort | Reply when convenient | Immediate conversation |
| Good for | FAQs, orders, simple qualification | Fast follow-up, appointment booking, older customers |
| Record kept | Written transcript | Call recording and transcript, subject to local law |
| Cost per conversation | Usually lower | Usually higher |

## Choose a chatbot first when

- Most of your enquiries already arrive by chat, web form or WhatsApp
- Questions are repetitive and have clear answers
- Customers want to reply in their own time
- You want a quick, low-risk starting point

## Choose a voice agent first when

- Your leads leave a phone number and expect a call
- Speed matters, and a call within minutes gets a better result than a message
- Your customers prefer talking, or are less comfortable typing
- The goal is booking a meeting or a viewing

## Many businesses use both

A common pattern is a chatbot for instant replies and basic questions, with a voice call triggered when a lead looks promising and has asked for contact.

## Questions to ask before you decide

1. Where do most of my leads come from today?
2. How fast do we respond, and what would faster be worth?
3. What do customers expect in my industry?
4. What rules apply to calling and messaging in the countries I sell into?

Pick one channel, run it on a single lead source, and measure the result before adding the other.
