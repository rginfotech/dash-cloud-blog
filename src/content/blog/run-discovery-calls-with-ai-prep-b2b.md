A discovery call is expensive. It uses a salesperson's time and the buyer's patience. When the first ten minutes are spent asking basic questions, both sides lose value.

## The problem with cold starts

Salespeople often join a call knowing only a name and a company. They spend time asking about team size, current tools and timing, which could have been collected beforehand.

## Let an AI agent do the pre-work

Before the call, the agent can:

- Ask the lead a few short questions through a form, chat or WhatsApp
- Capture the use case, team size, budget range and timeline
- Check the company website for basic context such as industry and offering
- Write a one-paragraph summary for the salesperson

## What a good summary contains

1. Who the lead is and what the company does
2. The problem they described in their own words
3. Key numbers such as volume, team size and timeline
4. Open questions to explore on the call
5. A suggested next step

## Keep the buyer's experience human

Be clear that the questions help the call be useful. Keep them short, never ask for sensitive information you do not need, and let the buyer skip anything.

## Measure the effect

Track how long discovery calls take, how many reach a next step and how often the salesperson says the summary was useful. Adjust the questions based on what they actually use.
