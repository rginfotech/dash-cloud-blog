B2B marketplaces send small suppliers a steady stream of enquiries. Buyers send the same request to many sellers and talk first to whoever answers well. A small team that is busy producing, packing and shipping rarely replies fast.

## What buyers want in the first reply

- Confirmation you can supply the product
- Price range or a clear way to get a quote
- Minimum order quantity and lead time
- A named person and a next step

## A reply workflow

1. **Capture** every enquiry from your marketplace, website form and email in one place.
2. **Reply instantly** with an AI-drafted answer using your catalogue and price rules.
3. **Ask two or three qualifying questions**: quantity, destination, timeline.
4. **Score the lead** and alert your sales owner for high-value enquiries.
5. **Follow up** automatically at day 3 and day 7 if there is no reply.

## Keep humans in the loop for quotes

Let AI handle the first response and information gathering. Have a person approve custom pricing, contracts and anything unusual. That keeps speed high without risking an incorrect commitment.

## Measure what matters

Track median first-response time, share of enquiries qualified, quotes sent and orders won by source. Review it monthly and prune the channels that bring unserious enquiries.
