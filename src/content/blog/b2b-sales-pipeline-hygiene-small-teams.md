A pipeline is only useful if it is true. Many small teams have deals that have not moved for months, missing next steps and forecasts based on hope. The result is surprises at the end of the quarter.

## Signs your pipeline needs cleaning

- Deals with no next step or no date
- Opportunities untouched for 30 days or more
- No clear stage definitions
- Different people record information differently

## A weekly 20-minute routine

1. **Review stale deals.** Anything without activity for two weeks gets a decision: follow up, move back or close.
2. **Check next steps.** Every open deal needs a next action with an owner and a date.
3. **Update stages honestly.** A stage should reflect what the buyer has done, not what you hope.
4. **Close lost deals with a reason.** Learn from them.

## What automation can do

- Remind owners when a deal has no next step
- Prompt a follow-up message after a quiet period
- Summarise the week's changes for the team lead
- Log emails and calls so nobody types them manually

## Keep it simple

You do not need a complex system. A shared sheet with clear stage definitions and a weekly review beats an expensive tool that nobody updates.

## Why it matters

A clean pipeline gives you a better forecast, helps you spot where deals get stuck and makes sure no warm lead is forgotten.
