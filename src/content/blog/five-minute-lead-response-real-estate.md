When someone fills in an enquiry form, they are usually filling in several. The agency that replies first, and replies usefully, starts the conversation. The others start by chasing.

## The speed-to-lead problem

Most teams cannot reply in minutes. Enquiries arrive at night, on weekends, and during site visits. The reply comes the next morning, by which point the buyer has already spoken to someone else.

Before raising your ad budget, check what happens to the leads you already pay for. Widely cited industry studies show response time has a strong effect on contact and qualification rates, so measure your own numbers rather than trusting any single figure.

## What a good first reply contains

- The buyer's name and the property they asked about
- A direct answer to the question they asked
- One qualifying question, such as budget or move-in timeline
- A clear next step: a call time or a viewing slot

## How AI closes the gap

An AI agent replies on WhatsApp, SMS, email or by phone within seconds, at any hour. It answers common questions, qualifies the lead, and hands a ready summary to your agent. Humans step in for negotiation and relationship, where they add the most value.

## Audit your speed this week

1. Submit a test enquiry to your own website, portals and ads.
2. Note how long the first reply takes, and how useful it is.
3. Repeat at night and on a weekend.
4. Fix the slowest channel first.

Faster replies make every lead source you already pay for work harder, which is usually cheaper than buying more traffic.
