Most real estate teams qualify leads in the same way: a salesperson calls, asks a few questions, and decides by gut feeling. An AI agent can ask the same questions every time, at any hour, and hand your team a clean summary.

The value is in the questions. Here are seven that work, and why each one matters.

## 1. What are you looking for?

Property type, bedrooms, and whether it is for living or investment. This sets the entire conversation and stops you sending the wrong listings.

## 2. Which areas are you considering?

Location is usually the strongest filter. Two preferred areas tell you far more than a general city name.

## 3. What is your budget range?

Ask for a range, not an exact number. People answer a range more honestly, and it keeps you from showing properties that will never fit.

## 4. When do you want to move or buy?

Timeline separates a buyer who needs to decide this month from someone exploring for next year. Both are valuable, but they need different follow-up.

## 5. How will you be paying?

Cash, mortgage, or sale of an existing property. You are not checking finances, just understanding how fast a deal could move.

## 6. Are you speaking with other agents?

This is a fair, friendly question. It tells you how urgent your response needs to be.

## 7. Can we book a viewing?

End with a clear next step. Offer two specific time slots rather than asking "when are you free?".

## How to score the answers

| Signal | Higher priority |
|---|---|
| Timeline | Within 3 months |
| Budget | Matches available listings |
| Viewing | Agreed a slot |
| Contact | Answered and replied |

Send the highest-scoring leads to a person immediately. Keep nurturing the rest automatically.

## Keep it conversational

Ask one question at a time, acknowledge each answer, and offer to skip anything the lead does not want to share. People disengage when qualification feels like a form.
