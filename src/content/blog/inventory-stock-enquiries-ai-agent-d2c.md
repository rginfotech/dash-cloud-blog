"Is this available in medium?" "When will it be back in stock?" These questions arrive all day, and they come from people who are close to buying. A slow answer sends them to a competitor.

## Why availability questions matter

A shopper who asks about stock has already chosen your product. They only need a clear answer. If nobody replies for hours, the interest fades.

## What the AI agent does

- **Checks live stock.** It reads availability from your store and answers for the exact size, colour or variant.
- **Suggests alternatives.** If a variant is out, it offers the closest option that is available.
- **Captures back-in-stock interest.** With permission, it records the customer's contact details and notifies them when the item returns.
- **Shares delivery estimates.** It answers how long shipping will take to the customer's location.

## Data you need in place

The agent is only as good as your data. Keep stock counts accurate, product names consistent and delivery times clearly defined. A short clean product sheet is better than a perfect but outdated one.

## Turn demand into planning

Every "notify me" request is a demand signal. If a product collects many of them, it is a strong candidate for restocking. Review the list before you place your next supplier order.

## Start small

Pick your five best-selling products, connect their stock data, and let the agent answer availability questions only. Measure how many conversations end in an order, then expand.
