An AI agent talks to your customers and sees their names, contact details and messages. That makes privacy part of the setup, not an afterthought. Good habits protect people and also build trust.

## Collect only what you need

Every extra question is extra risk. If you do not need a date of birth or an identity number to answer an enquiry, do not ask for it. Less data means less to protect.

## Be clear with customers

Tell people they are talking to an automated assistant and how their information will be used. A short, plain notice works better than a long legal document nobody reads.

## Know where the data goes

Ask your provider simple questions:

1. Where is the data stored?
2. Who can access it?
3. How long is it kept?
4. Is it used to train models for other customers?
5. Can customers ask for it to be deleted?

## Limit access inside your company

Only people who need conversation records should see them. Use individual logins and remove access when someone leaves.

## Keep sensitive details out

Do not let the agent collect payment card numbers, passwords or health details in chat unless your provider and process are designed for it and you understand the rules.

## Know the rules that apply

Privacy laws differ by country and may apply based on where your customers live. This article is general information, not legal advice. Speak with a qualified professional about your obligations.

## Review regularly

Look at a sample of conversations every month. Check for data that should not be there and fix the instructions that caused it.
