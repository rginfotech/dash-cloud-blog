More property buyers now look at homes in other cities and countries. They cannot visit on a whim, so they rely on your communication to decide whether to travel at all.

## What overseas buyers worry about

- Is the information accurate and current?
- Who can I trust to represent me?
- How does the process work from abroad?
- Will anyone reply when I am awake?

## Respond in their time zone

Your office hours may not match theirs. An AI agent can reply immediately and tell the buyer when a human agent will be available, stated in the buyer's own time zone. That removes the long gap that makes remote buyers anxious.

## Share the right details up front

Give clear information early:

1. Photos, floor plans and a video tour
2. The full price and any additional costs
3. A summary of the buying steps
4. The documents you will need to see

Do not give legal, tax or immigration advice. Direct buyers to qualified professionals for those topics, and note that rules differ by location.

## Offer flexible contact

Offer video calls at times that suit both sides, with the time zone written out. Some buyers prefer messaging apps, so ask which channel they like.

## Build trust with transparency

Be open about what is confirmed and what is not. Answer awkward questions directly. Remote buyers remember honest agents and recommend them.
