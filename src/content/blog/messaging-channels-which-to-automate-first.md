Customers contact businesses through many channels: website chat, messaging apps, email, social media and phone. You cannot automate everything at once, so choose one channel and do it well.

## Start where your customers already are

Look at where most enquiries come from today. A channel with high volume and repeated questions is usually the best first choice. Ask your team which channel creates the most repeated work.

## A quick comparison

- **Website chat.** Good for visitors with quick questions. Easy to launch.
- **Messaging apps.** Customers are comfortable there and replies are fast. Check the platform's rules and get consent for messages you send first.
- **Email.** Good for detailed requests, but replies are slower.
- **Phone.** Best for urgent or complex needs. Voice agents can handle common calls and pass on difficult ones.

## Questions to ask

1. Where do most enquiries arrive?
2. Where do customers expect fast answers?
3. Which channel has rules or limits we must follow?
4. Can our team monitor it during working hours?

## Keep the experience consistent

Whichever channels you use, give the same answers, the same tone and the same handover process. Customers should not get a different story depending on where they ask.

## Add channels gradually

Once the first channel runs smoothly, add the next one using what you learned. Reuse the same knowledge base so answers stay aligned.
