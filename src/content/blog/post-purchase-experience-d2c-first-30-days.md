Many online brands spend most of their effort getting the first order and very little on what happens afterwards. Yet the weeks after a purchase decide whether the customer returns, recommends you or asks for a refund.

## A simple 30-day timeline

**Day 0: Confirmation.** Confirm the order, show what was bought and explain what happens next.

**Day 1-2: Shipping update.** Share tracking and an honest delivery estimate.

**Delivery day: Arrival check.** A short message asking whether everything arrived safely.

**Day 5-7: Using the product.** Share a useful tip, a short guide or care instructions. Help the customer get value quickly.

**Day 10-14: Feedback.** Ask for a rating and, if happy, a review. Route unhappy replies to your team.

**Day 21-30: Next step.** Suggest a related product or a refill if it makes sense. Keep it relevant, not pushy.

## Where an AI agent helps

An agent can send these messages, answer questions such as "How do I use this?" and escalate problems to a person with the full context. Customers get help at the moment they need it.

## Keep it useful

Every message should help the customer. If it only tries to sell, they will tune out. Respect consent and offer an easy way to stop messages.

## Measure what matters

Track repeat purchase rate, support questions per order and review rate. Adjust the timing and content based on what actually changes behaviour.
