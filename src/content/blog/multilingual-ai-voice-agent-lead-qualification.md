Phone calls remain the fastest way to understand what a prospect needs. They are also the hardest part to scale: calls take time, happen outside office hours, and often need more than one language.

## What a voice agent does

An AI voice agent calls or answers calls, holds a natural conversation, and follows your qualification script. It can:

- Greet the lead and confirm they enquired
- Ask budget, timeline, and requirement questions
- Answer common questions from your knowledge base
- Book a meeting or demo in your calendar
- Send a written summary and transcript to your CRM

## Where it works best

- High-volume inbound enquiries that need an instant response
- Re-engaging older leads at scale
- Appointment reminders and confirmations
- Out-of-hours coverage and overflow when the team is busy

## Where humans stay in charge

Complex negotiation, sensitive topics and high-value relationships. Design a clean handoff so the agent passes context and the person never starts from zero.

## Responsible deployment checklist

- Tell people they are speaking with an AI.
- Respect do-not-call lists and local calling rules in each country.
- Get consent where required, and honour opt-outs immediately.
- Record and store calls only as your privacy policy and local law allow.
- Test with real scripts and accents before going live.

## How to pilot

Run a two to four week pilot on one lead source with a clear success metric, such as meetings booked per 100 leads. Compare against your current process, then decide whether to expand.
