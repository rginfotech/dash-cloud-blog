Small B2B teams rarely lack enquiries. They lack time. If every lead gets the same slow response, the best ones wait behind the worst ones.

You do not need an expensive CRM to fix this. A simple score is enough.

## Pick four signals

1. **Fit:** is this the kind of business you serve? Industry, company size, country.
2. **Intent:** did they ask for a quote or a demo, or just browse?
3. **Urgency:** do they have a date or deadline?
4. **Budget clarity:** have they shared a quantity, a range, or a target?

## Give each signal a score

For example, 0 to 3 points each, for a total out of 12.

| Score | What it means | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 9 to 12 | Strong lead | Personal reply within minutes |
| 5 to 8 | Possible | Automated reply, then follow-up within a day |
| 0 to 4 | Weak or unclear | Automated nurture only |

## Collect the signals automatically

Ask for them in the first reply. An AI agent can send an instant response and ask two or three short questions, such as the quantity needed and the delivery date. The answers fill your score without anyone typing.

## Keep the rules visible

Write the scoring rules on one page and share them with everyone who answers enquiries. Review them monthly. If your best customers keep scoring low, change the rules.

## Avoid these traps

- Too many signals, which makes scoring slow
- Never checking whether high scores actually become customers
- Treating a low score as a reason to ignore someone forever

Start simple, measure which scores convert, and refine. A spreadsheet that gets used beats a system that does not.
