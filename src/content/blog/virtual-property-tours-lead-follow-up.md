Virtual and video tours let buyers explore a property from anywhere, which is especially useful for overseas buyers and busy professionals. The tour itself is only half the job. What you do in the next hour decides whether it leads anywhere.

## The follow-up gap

Many agents publish a tour and wait. Viewers watch, think "interesting", and move on to the next listing. Without a prompt, even interested people forget.

## A simple follow-up flow

1. **Thank and ask.** Right after the tour, send a short message: "Thanks for taking the tour. What did you think of the layout?"
2. **Answer questions fast.** Common questions cover price, fees, nearby amenities and availability. An AI agent can answer these from your listing details.
3. **Offer the next step.** Suggest a live video call with an agent, or an in-person visit if the buyer is local.
4. **Send a summary.** Share the key facts and photos so the buyer can compare properties later.

## Make booking effortless

Offer two or three time slots instead of asking "When are you free?". Include the time zone clearly, especially for international buyers.

## Keep a record

Note which rooms and features each viewer spent time on if your tour tool provides it. It helps the agent have a more relevant conversation.

## Be honest about the property

A tour should be accurate and current. Mention anything not shown, such as work still to be completed. Trust built early makes later conversations easier.
