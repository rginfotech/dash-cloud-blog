A single rental listing can attract far more enquiries than a sale listing, and many are repeated questions. Property managers and landlords can spend hours answering the same messages.

## The most common rental questions

- Is it still available?
- What is the monthly rent and the deposit?
- What is included, such as utilities or furnishing?
- When can I move in, and what is the minimum lease?
- Are pets allowed?

An AI agent can answer all of these from the listing details, immediately and at any hour.

## Pre-screen politely

Beyond answering questions, the agent can ask a few practical questions: desired move-in date, number of occupants and length of stay. This helps match the right people to the right unit, and it avoids wasted viewings.

## Schedule viewings automatically

Offer open slots from the manager's calendar, confirm by message and send a reminder the day before. This reduces no-shows and saves back-and-forth.

## Keep a person in charge of decisions

Tenant selection must be fair and follow local housing and anti-discrimination laws. The agent can gather information and schedule, but decisions about applicants should be made by a person following your written criteria.

## Close the loop

Tell applicants clearly when a unit has been taken. A short, polite message keeps your reputation strong and your contacts available for the next listing.
