Small brands sell where their customers are: Instagram DMs, WhatsApp, email, a website chat. Each channel is another inbox someone has to watch, and every missed message is a missed sale.

## What an AI order-taking agent handles

- **Product questions**: sizes, materials, shipping times, ingredients
- **Recommendations** based on what the customer says they need
- **Order capture**: items, variants, address and payment link
- **Order status**: tracking links and delivery estimates
- **Handoff** to a human for complaints, custom requests and edge cases

## What it should not do

Good agents know their limits. They should not invent policies, promise discounts you have not approved, or keep arguing with an upset customer. Set clear rules and a visible way to reach a person.

## Set it up in five steps

1. Load your catalogue, FAQs, shipping and returns policy.
2. Define tone: friendly, brief, on brand.
3. Connect your store and payment link provider.
4. Set escalation rules, for example refunds or order value over a threshold.
5. Review real conversations every week and refine.

## Start small

Pick your busiest channel and your top ten questions. Measure response time, orders captured and escalation rate for two weeks. Expand only after the answers are accurate.
