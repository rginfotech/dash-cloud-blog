In many small businesses, one shared address such as "info@" receives everything: sales enquiries, supplier messages, invoices, support requests and spam. Important messages sit unread because nobody knows whose job it is.

## What goes wrong

- Hot sales leads wait behind routine messages
- Two people answer the same email, or nobody does
- Urgent issues are not seen until too late
- There is no record of response times

## A simple triage system

1. **Categorise.** Sort each message as sales, support, billing, supplier or other.
2. **Prioritise.** Mark urgent items and new prospects.
3. **Route.** Send each category to the right owner.
4. **Acknowledge.** Send a quick reply that confirms receipt and sets expectations.
5. **Track.** Record the time to first response.

## What an AI agent can do

An agent can read incoming messages, assign a category and priority, draft a first reply and notify the right person. It can also ask for missing information, such as an order number or quantity, before a human steps in.

## Keep people in control

Review the agent's categories regularly, especially at the start. Let staff correct mistakes so the system learns your business. Never let it send important replies, such as pricing, without approval.

## Protect privacy

Limit who can see sensitive messages and avoid forwarding personal data more widely than needed.

## Measure the result

Track first response time, the number of messages that were missed and how many enquiries become customers. A tidy inbox should show up in the numbers.
