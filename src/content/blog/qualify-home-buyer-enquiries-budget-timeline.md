Property portals and ads bring a steady stream of enquiries. Some are ready to buy within weeks. Others are just curious. Treating them all the same wastes agent time and delays the people who matter.

## Three signals that matter most

1. **Budget.** A rough range is enough. It tells you which properties to show.
2. **Timeline.** Buying this month and buying next year need different conversations.
3. **Finance status.** Whether the buyer has financing arranged or still needs to explore it.

## Ask naturally, not like a form

An AI agent can reply to a new enquiry within a minute and ask one friendly question at a time. For example: "Thanks for your interest. Are you looking to move in the next few months?" Short, conversational questions get more answers than long forms.

## Sort into three groups

- **Ready now.** Clear budget, near-term timeline. Hand to an agent straight away with a summary.
- **Planning.** Interested but later. Add to a nurture sequence with useful updates.
- **Just browsing.** Send helpful content and check back later.

## Respect the buyer

Make every question optional and explain why you ask. Never press for financial details beyond what is needed to suggest suitable properties. Be clear about how you will use their information.

## What the agent should not do

It should not give financial or legal advice, promise approval for a loan or guess at prices it has not been given. Those answers come from a qualified person.

## Review and refine

After a month, compare which scored groups turned into viewings and offers. Adjust the questions and the thresholds based on real results.
