Every real estate team has the same hidden asset: a spreadsheet of past enquiries nobody has touched in months. Each one paid for with ad spend, portal fees or a salesperson's time, and then left to go cold.

## Why old leads go quiet

Agents prioritise the hottest enquiry of the day. Everyone else waits for a follow-up that never comes. The buyer did not lose interest. They got busy, compared options, or were never asked the right question.

## What an AI agent does differently

An AI follow-up agent works the whole list, not just the top of it.

- **Calls or messages each lead** within hours of you uploading the list, in a natural tone.
- **Asks qualifying questions**: budget, timeline, preferred area, property type, and whether they are still looking.
- **Handles common questions** about price ranges, availability and location without waiting for an agent.
- **Books a site visit** directly into your team's calendar when the lead is interested.
- **Logs everything** back to your CRM so your agents see the full context before the first call.

## A simple 4-step playbook

1. **Export** leads older than 30 days with name, phone, source and original enquiry.
2. **Segment** them: enquired about a specific project, general enquiry, or already visited.
3. **Write one clear reason to reach out**: a new phase launch, a price update, a limited unit release.
4. **Review results weekly**: replies, qualified leads, visits booked, and objections you keep hearing.

## What to measure

| Metric | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Contact rate | Are the numbers and channels still valid? |
| Qualified rate | How many are genuinely still in market? |
| Visits booked | The outcome that turns into revenue |
| Cost per booked visit | Compare with your paid-ad cost per visit |

## Set expectations honestly

Not every old lead will revive, and results depend on list quality, offer and market. The point is that reaching all of them costs a fraction of buying new leads, and your sales team only spends time on people who said yes.

Always follow the calling and messaging rules in the country you operate in, and make it easy for people to opt out.
