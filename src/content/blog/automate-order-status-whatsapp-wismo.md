For many online stores, the most common support question is also the simplest: "Where is my order?" Each one is easy to answer, and together they take hours of your team's time every week.

## Why customers ask

They are not being difficult. They want reassurance. When there is silence after paying, anxiety rises, and a message to support is the fastest way to resolve it.

## Answer before they ask

Send proactive updates at the key moments:

1. Order confirmed
2. Order packed and ready to ship
3. Shipped, with the tracking link
4. Out for delivery
5. Delivered, with a request for feedback

Each message should be short, and include the order number and a clear next step.

## Let an AI agent handle the rest

Some customers will still ask. An AI agent can read the order number, check the status in your store or courier system, and reply right away with the latest status and expected date. If the order is delayed or stuck, it can explain what is happening and alert a person.

## Handle problems honestly

Delays happen. Tell customers early, say what you are doing, and offer a choice where you can. A customer who is told about a delay usually stays happier than one who finds out by chasing.

## What to track

| Metric | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Order status tickets per 100 orders | Is automation working? |
| First response time | Faster is better |
| Share handled without a person | How much load you have removed |
| Delivery complaints | Where your couriers need attention |

## Start small

Connect one courier and one channel. Send updates for a week, review the questions that still arrive, and improve the messages based on them.
