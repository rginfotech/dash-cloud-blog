A project launch is the busiest time for a property developer. Ads go live, portals refresh, and enquiries arrive faster than the sales team can answer them. The danger is not a lack of interest. It is that serious buyers wait behind curious browsers.

## What goes wrong during a launch

- Enquiries arrive from many places: ads, portals, your website, social media and walk-ins.
- Replies slow down just when speed matters most.
- Leads sit in different spreadsheets and inboxes.
- The sales team cannot tell who is ready to buy.

## What an AI agent handles

**Instant first reply.** Every enquirer gets an answer within seconds, on the channel they used.

**Project questions.** Unit types, sizes, price ranges, payment plans, amenities, location and handover timeline, answered from approved information only.

**Qualification.** Budget, preferred unit, timeline and financing, asked conversationally.

**Site-visit booking.** Offers real slots and confirms them, with a reminder before the visit.

**Clean handoff.** Your sales team receives a summary and a priority, not a raw list.

## Prepare before launch day

1. Collect approved answers: pricing, floor plans, payment schedule, legal and regulatory details.
2. Decide which questions must go to a person, such as negotiation and custom offers.
3. Set the viewing calendar and who covers each slot.
4. Test the agent with real questions from your last launch.

## Be careful with promises

Pricing and availability change quickly during a launch. Keep the agent's information current, and have it say "I will confirm that with our team" rather than guess.

## After the spike

Keep following up. Many buyers decide weeks after the first enquiry. Automated follow-up keeps those leads warm without extra effort from your team.
