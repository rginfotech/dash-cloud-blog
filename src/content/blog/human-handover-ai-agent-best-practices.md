The quality of an AI agent is judged not only by what it answers, but by how well it knows when to stop. A smooth handover to a person is what turns a risky automation into a trusted one.

## When to hand over

- The customer asks for a person
- The customer is upset or frustrated
- The question involves money, legal issues or safety
- The agent is unsure or has failed to help twice
- The customer is a high-value or long-standing account

## How to hand over well

1. **Be honest.** Say that you are connecting the customer to a team member.
2. **Pass the context.** The person should see the whole conversation and a short summary.
3. **Set expectations.** Say how long it will take and how the customer will be contacted.
4. **Do not make them repeat.** Nothing frustrates people more than telling the story again.

## Make sure someone is there

A handover is useless if nobody picks it up. Assign owners, set response targets and make sure there is a fallback outside working hours, such as a clear message about when the team will respond.

## Learn from handovers

Every handover is a lesson. If many are about the same question, add that answer to the agent's knowledge. Review them weekly and shrink the list over time.

## Offer the choice

Always let customers reach a person if they want to. People accept automation more readily when they know they are not trapped in it.
