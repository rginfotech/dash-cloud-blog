AI agents work best when they are given a clear job, good information and sensible limits. This checklist helps you find out whether your business is ready, and where to start.

## The checklist

1. **You have a specific problem.** For example, slow replies to enquiries or too many repeat questions. "Use AI" is not a goal.
2. **You can measure it today.** Response time, bookings, tickets or conversion. A baseline lets you prove the result.
3. **The task is repetitive.** The same questions or steps happen many times a week.
4. **You have clear answers.** Prices, policies, availability and FAQs are written down and correct.
5. **There is a defined handoff.** You know which situations must go to a person, and who that is.
6. **Your data is accessible.** The agent can connect to your CRM, store, calendar or spreadsheets.
7. **Customers already use the channel.** Chat, WhatsApp, phone or email where your customers prefer to reach you.
8. **You have an owner.** One person is responsible for reviewing conversations and improving the agent.
9. **You understand the rules.** Consent, privacy, calling and messaging rules in the countries you serve.
10. **You can run a small pilot.** One source, one team, four weeks, with a clear success number.

## How to read your score

- **8 to 10 ticks:** you are ready. Start a pilot.
- **5 to 7 ticks:** you are close. Fix the gaps first, mainly the written answers and the handoff.
- **Fewer than 5:** begin with groundwork. Define the problem, collect your information and measure the baseline.

## Choosing your first project

Pick the one with the clearest pain and the lowest risk. Common first projects are instant replies to new enquiries, order status questions, and appointment reminders.

## What not to do

Do not try to automate everything at once, and do not remove human help entirely. The best results usually come from the agent handling routine work, while people handle the conversations that need judgement.
