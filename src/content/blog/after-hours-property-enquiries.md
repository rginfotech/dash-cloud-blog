People browse properties when they are free, which is usually evenings and weekends. If your first reply comes on Monday morning, the buyer has probably contacted three other agents by then.

## What happens to late enquiries

- They wait until the next working day
- The reply arrives after the buyer has lost interest
- No one knows which leads were most urgent

## A better approach

An AI agent can reply immediately, whatever the hour. It does not replace your agents. It holds the conversation until a person is available.

### What it can do at night

1. Acknowledge the enquiry and thank the buyer
2. Answer basic questions about the listing
3. Ask two or three qualifying questions
4. Offer viewing times for the coming days
5. Leave a clear summary for the agent's morning

## Set honest expectations

Tell the buyer what will happen next: "An agent will contact you tomorrow morning at 9." A clear promise is better than silence, as long as you keep it.

## Prioritise the morning list

Start each day with a ranked list of overnight enquiries, with the hottest leads at the top. Agents spend their first hour on people who are ready to act.

## Keep it human

If a buyer wants to speak to a person immediately, make that easy and explain when someone will be available.
