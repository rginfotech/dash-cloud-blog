You may have heard that AI agents will transform business. Strip away the hype and the idea is simple: an AI agent is software that can understand a request, decide what to do and take action on your behalf, within limits you set.

## How it differs from a basic chatbot

A traditional chatbot follows a fixed script. If the customer says something unexpected, it gets stuck. An AI agent understands natural language, uses your business information to answer and can complete tasks such as booking a meeting or updating an order.

## The three parts of an agent

1. **Understanding.** It reads or listens to what a person says and works out what they want.
2. **Knowledge.** It uses information you provide, such as product details, policies and prices.
3. **Action.** It does something useful: replies, asks a follow-up question, creates a record or hands over to a person.

## Where businesses use them first

- Answering common customer questions
- Responding to new enquiries quickly
- Qualifying leads before a salesperson speaks to them
- Sending reminders and follow-ups
- Collecting information for quotes or bookings

## What agents are not

They are not a replacement for good judgement. They can make mistakes, so important decisions, refunds and sensitive conversations should involve a person.

## How to start

Choose one repeating task that is slow today. Write down the questions and rules clearly. Test the agent with real examples and keep a person in the loop. Grow from there.
