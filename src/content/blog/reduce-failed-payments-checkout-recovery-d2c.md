Abandoned carts get a lot of attention, but failed payments are an even warmer problem. The shopper has chosen the product, entered their details and tried to pay. Something went wrong, and many simply give up.

## Common reasons payments fail

- A mistyped card number or expiry date
- The bank declines an unfamiliar online payment
- A technical timeout during the process
- A required verification step that the customer did not complete
- A payment method that is not supported in their country

## A helpful recovery flow

1. **Detect the failure.** Your payment provider shows which attempts failed and why, where permitted.
2. **Reach out quickly.** Send a calm, short message: "Your payment did not go through. Your cart is saved. Here is a link to try again."
3. **Offer help.** Let the customer ask questions or try another payment method.
4. **Escalate when needed.** Repeated failures may need a person to investigate.

## Keep it safe

Never ask customers to send card numbers, security codes or passwords in a chat message. Direct them to your secure checkout page only. Keep messages free of sensitive payment details.

## Fix the root causes

Review failure reasons each month. If one payment method fails often, or one country has problems, fix the checkout itself. Reducing failures at the source helps every customer.

## Measure recovery

Track how many failed attempts become completed orders after your message. This is a direct, easy number to improve.
